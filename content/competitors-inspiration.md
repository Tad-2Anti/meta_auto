## Competitor watch

- Other “AI Instagram agent” or content-ops tools
- Pure humanizer / caption tools
- Browser-automation posters (risk note: ToS)

## How to use

1. Collect 10–12 recent Reels per account (manual, human speed).
2. Note views + estimated median.
3. Flag anything ≥3x median.
4. Classify hook formula.
5. Write swipe.md. Re-rank monthly.

Raw views are not evidence. A 2M account doing 400k is a quiet day. A 4k account doing 400k found something.
