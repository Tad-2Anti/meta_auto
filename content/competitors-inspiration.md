- Tools and accounts around ReelDrop, ManyChat, Claude-based agents, n8n automation workflows, AI avatar Reels systems.

## Neutral formats that are working right now (for first posts)
- Educational tip Reels (3–5 concrete steps, strong hook, on-screen text)
- “What the data says” / ranking signals (watch time, sends, originality)
- Systems / process breakdowns (how to score a hook, weekly plan, etc.)
- Before/after or “I tested this”
- Faceless or text-heavy education with high retention
- Avoid pure entertainment, watermarks, or high-volume copy-paste early on

## How to use for first posts
1. Pick 3–5 neutral tip formats from the list.
2. Draft scripts using the existing first-reel style (hook → stake → body → loop).
3. Humanize, score, then post manually.
4. After 5–10 posts, run full outlier analysis on these accounts and our own.
